TITAN GAS FIREPLACE - LANDING PAGE (live version)
==================================================

WHAT THIS IS
The finished gas fireplace landing page for Titan Chimney & Fireplace.
Upload the whole folder exactly as it is. index.html, the "img" folder
and the "js" folder must stay together in the same place.

HOW TO PUT IT LIVE
1. Pick the address, for example: https://YOURDOMAIN.com/gas-fireplace/
2. On the hosting, make that folder (in this example: gas-fireplace).
3. Upload index.html, img/ and js/ into it. Do not rename anything.
4. Open the address on a phone and check: photos show, the map works,
   the Call buttons dial (425) 307-9165.
5. Check speed at https://pagespeed.web.dev (Mobile).

GOOGLE ADS
- Final URL = the https address of the folder above.
- Google tag: open index.html and paste the Google tag (gtag.js) snippet
  where it says  <!-- GOOGLE TAG: paste ... here -->  near the top.
- Call tracking ("Calls from website"): every phone link on the page is
  tel:+14253079165 and shows (425) 307-9165, so Google's forwarding-number
  snippet can swap them all. Count calls of 60 seconds or more.
- Also set up "Calls from ads" (call assets), counted from 60 seconds.
- Optional backup conversion "Click to call": create it in Google Ads
  (Goals > Conversions > + Create conversion action > Website > add manually,
  category "Phone call lead", Count: One) and send its label to the owner;
  it gets built into the page. Set it to "Secondary" so bidding uses the real calls.
- Hosting: turn on gzip or Brotli compression (most hosts have it on already).
  The page is about 4x smaller compressed, and Google counts speed toward
  the landing page experience score.
- City name in the headline: in Google Ads, at the account or campaign level,
  open Settings > Campaign URL options > Final URL suffix and paste exactly:
      loc={loc_physical_ms}&iloc={loc_interest_ms}
  The page then shows "Gas Fireplace Repair in Kent" (or whichever of our
  60 cities the click is from). Anyone else sees "Seattle-Area Gas Fireplace
  Repair". Test: open the page with ?city=Kent at the end of the address.
- Ad schedule: Sun-Fri 7:30 AM - 10:30 PM only. Closed Saturday.
- Location: people IN or regularly in the area, Marysville area to Tacoma area
  (60 cities in Snohomish, King and Pierce counties, listed on the page).
