Questions

Gas Fireplace FAQ

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. The inspection is free, with no obligation. We check your fireplace, show you what we find, and give you a written price if anything needs work. You decide from there.

How do I get scheduled?

Call (425) 307-9165. A real person answers, asks a few questions about your fireplace, and books a time that works for you while you're on the phone.

Who should be home for the visit?

Anyone 18 or older can let us in. If someone else in the home helps make decisions, it's best to have them there or available by phone. That way we can walk everyone through what we find and answer pricing questions in one visit.

Why won't my gas fireplace pilot stay lit?

The most common causes are a worn thermocouple or thermopile, a dirty pilot, or a weak gas supply to the pilot. Call us and we'll find the cause during your free inspection.

Do you give a price before you start?

Yes. You get a written, flat-rate price after the inspection. Nothing starts until you approve it.

What parts do you use?

We use OEM (factory) parts whenever they're available for your fireplace, so the repair fits and lasts the way the manufacturer intended.

Can you convert my wood fireplace to gas?

Yes. We handle the whole project: permits, plans, parts, gas line, venting, and installation, then test everything before we leave.

How often should a gas fireplace be serviced?

Once a year is the standard recommendation, ideally before the heating season. A yearly service keeps it lighting reliably and lets us catch worn parts early.

I smell gas. What should I do?

Leave the house right away. Don't flip switches or use the fireplace. Call 911 or your gas company from outside. Once it's safe, call us to find and fix the problem.
