Questions People Ask Before They Call

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. No charge and no obligation. We check your fireplace, show you what we find, and give you a written price if anything needs work. You decide from there.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. Most people who call us want their fireplace fixed, so a free, honest inspection is how we earn the job. If it doesn't need work, we tell you that.

How do I get scheduled?

Call (425) 307-9165. A real person answers, asks a few questions about your fireplace, and books a time that works for you while you're on the phone.

Do you come to my area?

We cover 60 cities from the Marysville area down to the Tacoma area, across Snohomish, King, and Pierce counties: from Arlington and Lake Stevens through Seattle and the Eastside to Kent, Puyallup, and Gig Harbor. Not sure? Call and ask.

Are you really a local company?

Yes. Titan started in Seattle and grew from there. We're one company with our own office, our own team, and one phone number. We don't sell your information or pass your call to whoever is free. The person who answers works for Titan, and so does the technician who comes to your home.

Do you work on condo and townhome fireplaces?

Yes. We work on direct-vent gas fireplaces in condos and townhomes. If your building has rules for contractors, tell us when you call and we'll follow them.

Who should be home for the visit?

Anyone 18 or older can let us in. If someone else in the home helps decide, it's best to have them there or on the phone, so we can walk everyone through what we find and answer pricing questions in one visit.

Why won't my gas fireplace pilot stay lit?

The most common causes are a worn thermocouple or thermopile, a dirty pilot, or a weak gas supply to the pilot. We'll find the cause during your free inspection.

Is my old fireplace worth fixing?

Usually, yes. We've restored fireplaces close to 40 years old. We'll tell you honestly if a repair makes sense or if replacing it is the better buy.

Do you give a price before you start?

Yes. You get a written, flat-rate price after the inspection. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Can you convert my wood fireplace to gas?

Yes. We handle the whole project: permits, plans, parts, gas line, venting, and installation, then test everything before we leave.

Is a smell normal the first time we turn it on in the fall?

A light, dusty smell for the first hour of the season is common. It's dust burning off the burner. If the smell is strong, keeps coming back, or the glass turns black, have it checked. If it smells like rotten eggs, that's gas. See the next question.

I smell gas. What should I do?

Leave the house right away. Don't flip switches or use the fireplace. Call 911 or your gas company from outside. Once it's safe, call us to find and fix the problem.
